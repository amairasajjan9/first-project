Breakthrough Challenge

A shortlist of candidate research topics in advanced photonics and metamaterials being considered for this project — each explores how engineered structure, rather than raw material, can produce physically unusual behaviour.

01.

Candidate Topics

01

Negative Refraction & Metamaterials

Engineering materials that make light bend in unusual, seemingly "wrong" directions.

02

Photonic Time Crystals

Materials whose properties change periodically in time, causing light to behave in strange ways.

03

Bound States in the Continuum

Waves that somehow remain trapped even though they exist within a range of frequencies where they should be able to escape.

04

Thermal Cloaking

Designing materials that redirect heat around an object, making the object difficult for surrounding temperature sensors to detect.

05

Topological Photonics

Engineering structures that force light to travel along special, highly protected pathways.

06

Mechanical Metamaterials

Materials whose unusual properties come from their engineered geometry rather than what they are made of.
